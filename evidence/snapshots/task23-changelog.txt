2026-07-05
DAO Proposal: Increase Governance Accessibility and Protect Contributor Privacy
NOT APPLIED
Voted to remove the voting whitelist requirement and lift geographic restrictions for general DAO members, keeping them for High Council and DAO Leadership.
VOTE RECORD
For at 53.8%, basic ballot, closed 2026-07-05, 119.96 hours. Open on Snapshot
CLAUSE IT TOUCHES
Section 2.3 Voting Power, which states voting power is proportional to RBNT held or delegated. The ratified constitution contains no whitelist, no eligibility list and no geographic restriction, so this removes something the constitution never imposed.
APPLIED? NO
The whitelist strategy is still attached to the space and to both proposals created after the vote closed. The last signed settings change is 2026-01-28, five months before this vote, so no configuration change was made in response to it.
NOTE
Two layers of finding here. First, it passed and was not applied. Second, the thing it asked to remove was never a restriction: the whitelist strategy returns 1 point for a listed address and 0 otherwise, against RBNT balances in the millions. On the most recent proposal, 15 of 27 voters were not on the whitelist and cast 26.7 percent of all voting power. Voter eligibility is governed by voteValidation, which has been set to any for the entire life of the space.

Amendment to Leadership Structure

2026-07-24
High Council Elections
APPLIED
Elected 3 High Council seats. Winners by score: Buffy, NekoyaManeki, Light.
VOTE RECORD
Buffy at 32.9%, copeland ballot, closed 2026-07-24, 72 hours. Open on Snapshot
CLAUSE IT TOUCHES
Section 2.1 Governance Phases, which places the High Council at Phase 0. No election procedure exists anywhere in the ratified text.
APPLIED? YES
The vote closed with a decided ranking.
WHAT IS DISPUTED
This proposal replaced an earlier High Council election that was deleted. Its own body states the vote 'has been reset with a corrected voting method' because ranked choice 'is designed to elect a single winner' and could not fill 3 seats, and that 'The new proposal uses Approval voting'.
RESOLUTION
The remedy described was not the remedy applied. The replacement proposal runs ballot type copeland, not approval. Copeland is a pairwise Condorcet method, not the tick-every-candidate-you-support count the body promises voters. Snapshot supports approval as a distinct type, so this was a choice rather than a limitation. The election result stands, but voters were told the ballot worked differently from how it actually worked.
NOTE
The superseded proposal is absent from the archive. The space carries 50 proposal messages against 30 live proposals, so 20 proposals have been deleted. Deleted content remains on IPFS.
