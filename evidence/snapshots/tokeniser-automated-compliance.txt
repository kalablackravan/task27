THE PROBLEM TODAY

Manual checks and late detection

Investor eligibility, holder limits and transfer restrictions are typically managed through spreadsheets, emails and manual sign-offs. Breaches are often identified after the trade, when remediation is slow and costly.

Lower compliance cost.

HOW IT WORKS

Checked before
every trade

Before any trade proceeds, the investor's identity, eligibility (including wholesale or sophisticated status where required) and your limits are verified automatically. Trades that fail a check do not proceed, and every check is logged.
