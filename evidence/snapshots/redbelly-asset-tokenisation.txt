Tokenisation is becoming the foundation of modern financial markets
Built for a world of AI-driven markets

The next wave of market activity will be driven by intelligent agents, not just people.

Redbelly enables both individuals and AI agents to interact with tokenised investment products, with identity, permissions, and compliance applied at the point of transaction.

This ensures that as markets become more automated, they remain trusted, verifiable, and globally accessible.

Asset issuers can issue and distribute products globally, with compliance built into the infrastructure

A network of clean liquidity, combined with verifiable credentials enable tokenised investment products with defined investor eligibility, enforced at the point of trade.

AML, CTF, and Travel Rule requirements can be applied as needed, supporting jurisdictional compliance without restricting access at the network level.

Issuers reach global markets. Investors access products they are eligible for. Secondary liquidity remains aligned with regulatory requirements.

Build and manage bespoke portfolios

Construct portfolios across tokenised investment products, aligned to defined objectives, constraints, and eligibility requirements. Portfolios can be monitored and rebalanced dynamically, either directly or through AI-driven agents.

Redbelly is Digital Public Token Infrastructure

Redbelly is designed to meet the requirements of regulated financial markets, providing open infrastructure that enables institutions to issue, manage, and distribute digital financial assets at scale.

Finality - Deterministic finality ensures transactions are confirmed once and cannot be reversed, providing certainty for financial markets
Accountability - Verified participants enable full auditability and clear accountability across all transactions
Security - Formally verified consensus eliminates forks and rollbacks, ensuring consistent and reliable execution
Performance - High throughput infrastructure supports real world volumes with speed, reliability and no transaction loss
Compliance - Designed to meet regulatory requirements across jurisdictions, enabling compliant issuance and distribution of financial products
