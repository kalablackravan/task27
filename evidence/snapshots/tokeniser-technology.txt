FAQ

Common questions about the technology beneath Tokeniser answered.

Is Redbelly a private network?

No. Redbelly is a public network designed to meet the three statutory tests for public digital token infrastructure under Australian law: open and non-discretionary, permissionless contribution, and no critical participant.

What does “formally verified” mean here?

Redbelly’s consensus protocol is proven correct using model checking, an automated method that exhaustively checks every reachable state of the protocol.

Do I need a new licence to use the infrastructure?

No. The protocol layer is designed to sit outside markets, clearing and settlement, and custody licensing. Your existing AFSL obligations, issuance, disclosure and conduct, remain unchanged.

How fast does it settle?

Settlement is quasi-instant. Deterministic consensus finalises a transaction in a single round, with finality the moment it completes.

Where did the technology come from?

Redbelly was born from research at the University of Sydney and CSIRO, Australia’s government science agency, with a patented consensus method built from scratch.

Can it connect to other networks?

Yes. Full EVM compatibility gives connection to more than 150 networks out of the box, with permissioned or permissionless access as your mandate allows.
