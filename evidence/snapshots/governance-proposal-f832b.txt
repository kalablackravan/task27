A proposal that is not approved may be resubmitted at any time where it has been materially revised in response to community feedback.
Material revisions may include changes to objectives, scope, implementation, budget, governance impacts, risk mitigation, KPIs, timeline or other substantive elements.
A proposal that is substantially unchanged should normally not be resubmitted until a reasonable period has elapsed or circumstances have materially changed.
Proposal authors are encouraged to include a summary of changes made since the previous submission.
Proposal reviewers may recommend further consultation before a resubmitted proposal proceeds to voting.
Nothing in this policy prevents urgent constitutional, security or treasury matters from being reconsidered where circumstances require.
This policy promotes continuous improvement rather than preventing community consideration of revised ideas.
